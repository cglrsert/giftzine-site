Questions

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and 5 to 20 photos. Magazines are 8 to 20 pages.

Is there a subscription?

No. GiftZine is a single purchase with every collection and feature included, and you can make unlimited magazines.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Photos are analyzed and stored on your iPhone and never uploaded. Nothing leaves the device unless you export it.

Can I print it?

Yes. Export a print-ready 6 × 9 in PDF and print it at home or with any print shop.

Which languages?

The app and every magazine are available in English and Turkish.
